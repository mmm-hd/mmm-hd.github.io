Profile

I studied statistics (B.Sc., M.Sc.) and psychology (B.Sc.) at the University of Vienna and obtained a PhD in Computational Linguistics from Heidelberg University. I have worked in the field of medical statistics for over 15 years, contributing to 50+ successful collaborations with medical partners across a wide range of fields.

Statistical Consulting

If you are interested in a statistical consulting session, please contact me.

Research Interests

My research interests include:

  • Applied statistics and data science (with applications in medicine, psychiatry, epidemiology, bioinformatics, molecular biology, and meteorology)

  • Application and evaluation of artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques in the life sciences

  • Methods for time-to-event analysis

Teaching

I teach biomathematics and epidemiology to medical students at the Medical Faculty Mannheim of Heidelberg University, as well as in the faculty's Translational Medical Research Master's program.
