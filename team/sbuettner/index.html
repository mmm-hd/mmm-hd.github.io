Profile

I have been working at our Core Facility at the Mannheim Medical Faculty since 1985, where I am responsible for organizational tasks (office administration, organizing lectures and seminars, placing orders, etc.) and assist physicians and students with data analysis for studies and dissertations.

This includes
Creating Moodle courses for Excel, Word and our Statistical Software SAS
Data management
Conducting descriptive statistics
Applying regression analyses and Kaplan-Meier and Cox models
Determining risks (e.g. odds ratios)
Using agreement analysis (e.g., Kappa and Bland-Altman analysis)
